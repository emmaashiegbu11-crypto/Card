## 2026-07-09 - [Interactive Demo Loopability]
**Learning:** For minimal component demos, implementing an "empty state" loop (e.g., a "Restore" button after a toast expires) significantly improves the developer/reviewer experience by allowing repeated interaction without manual page refreshes.
**Action:** Always provide a 'reset' or 'restore' capability in interactive demos to allow users to explore micro-interactions multiple times seamlessly.
